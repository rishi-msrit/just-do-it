About This Project

Privacy Disclosure (Layman Terms): This website runs completely in your local web browser. None of your inputs, text, scores, or results are ever sent to a server, stored in a database, or tracked by anyone. Your personal reflections are completely private, local, and secure on your own machine.

Who made this

I am a third-year engineering student. Between coursework, project deadlines, and trying to plan the early phases of my career, I found myself repeatedly frozen by choices. I wanted a way to systematically process my thoughts, and so I compiled the tools that worked for me into this notebook.

Why this exists

We are constantly subjected to information overload. When you need to make a decision, typical web tools try to hide the complexity behind flashy AI inputs that spit out answers for you. Personally, struggling with focus, ADHD, and analysis paralysis, I didn’t want an algorithm to tell me what to do. I wanted a clean, quiet space to run standard analytical frameworks for myself.

This is a zero-tracking, zero-database static site. None of your data goes to a server. It exists entirely on your browser, so you can think in absolute privacy.

Philosophy

"This site doesn't think for you. It hands you the lens, you do the looking."

A framework doesn't give you a magical answer; it forces you to categorize your information so you can see your own biases. By structuring your pros and cons, reach-impact metrics, or root causes, you gain clarity on what steps to take next.

The tools

The 18 tools included here are not proprietary. They are established frameworks developed in business strategy, software planning, psychology, and personal development over decades. SWOT, Eisenhower matrices, Fishbone diagrams, and Ikigai are mental tools that belong in the public domain. This toolkit simply makes them accessible, printable, and easy to interact with.

Privacy & Telemetry Disclosure

This application functions entirely as a client-side execution block. There are no backend database servers, tracking cookies, user accounts, or tracking telemetry packages compiled within this site.

When you click "Build my tool" or load a model, all mathematical formulations, grid sorting algorithms, and text rendering are executed on your device's local CPU. If you close the tab, your input is temporarily saved only in your local browser's storage space (`localStorage`) for your convenience, and can be cleared at any time. We collect no logs.

What's next

This is an ongoing compilation. If there is a reflection exercise or decision matrix you find yourself using repeatedly that isn't featured here, please share it. I plan to refine the visuals, add more tools, and improve the PDF export layouts over time.
