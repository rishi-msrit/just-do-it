RICE Scoring Framework

What it is

RICE is an objective scoring model developed by product messaging company Intercom to help teams prioritize features, ideas, or projects. Rather than relying on gut feelings or the opinions of the loudest person in the room, RICE calculates a single score for each item using four criteria: Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort. The resulting table lets you compare vastly different ideas on an objective, numbers-based playing field.

The 4 Scoring Variables

  • Reach (R): The number of users or transactions this project will affect in a given timeframe (e.g., "500 customers per quarter").
  • Impact (I): How much this project contributes to your goal for each individual it reaches. Intercom uses a standard multiplier scale:
    • 3 = Massive Impact
    • 2 = High Impact
    • 1 = Medium Impact
    • 0.5 = Low Impact
    • 0.25 = Minimal Impact
  • Confidence (C): How sure are you about your Reach, Impact, and Effort numbers? Rated as a percentage:
    • 100% = High Confidence (backed by customer data or direct metrics).
    • 80% = Medium Confidence (backed by secondary research or sibling projects).
    • 50% = Low Confidence (speculative, guess, or placeholder). Avoid starting projects with less than 50% confidence.
  • Effort (E): The total work required from all team members (developers, designers, marketers) calculated in person-weeks or hours (e.g., 2 person-weeks).

The RICE Formula

RICE Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence %) / Effort

A high RICE score represents a high-return project that can be built quickly with high certainty. Always sort descending and prioritize items scoring near the top.

Real-world Examples

Example A (Self-Serve Invoice Downloads):
• Reach: 10,000 customers per month who request invoices manually.
• Impact: 2 (High - saves customer friction and support load).
• Confidence: 100% (based on support tickets logs).
• Effort: 2 person-weeks of dev work.
• RICE Score: (10,000 × 2 × 1.0) / 2 = 10,000.
Example B (Complete Database System Migration):
• Reach: 15,000 total customers (affects everyone, but invisibly).
• Impact: 0.5 (Low - checkout load speed increases by only 3%).
• Confidence: 50% (untested performance metrics).
• Effort: 8 person-weeks of DevOps work.
• RICE Score: (15,000 × 0.5 × 0.5) / 8 = 468.75.
