WOOP Goal Setter

What it is

WOOP (Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan) is a science-backed mental strategy developed by NYU psychologist Gabriele Oettingen. It is based on twenty years of research in the science of motivation. Unlike traditional "positive thinking," which can actually drain our energy by tricking our brain into feeling like we've already won, WOOP uses mental contrasting—coupling our desires directly with the internal obstacles holding us back—to pre-program our brains for immediate action.

The 4 Steps of WOOP

1. Wish (W): Define a goal that is challenging but achievable for you in the near term.

2. Outcome (O): Visualize the best-case result. How will it feel? What is the core benefit?

3. Obstacle (O): Identify the internal obstacle holding you back. This is critical. It must be something within your control (e.g., procrastination, fear of failure, social anxiety) rather than external excuses.

4. Plan (P): Create an If-Then script. If the internal obstacle arises, what action will you take to pivot? (e.g., "If I start browsing social media, then I will close the tab and write one line of code").

Real-world Examples

Example A (Public Speaking):
• Wish: Pitch my project to the leadership committee.
• Outcome: Securing funding and professional validation.
• Obstacle: Social anxiety, freezing up, and heart racing.
• Plan: If I start feeling my chest tighten and forget my next slide, then I will pause, take 3 deep breaths, and refer to my physical index cue cards.
Example B (Learning & Code):
• Wish: Finish the first module of my online programming course.
• Outcome: Feeling confident to start building my own web applications.
• Obstacle: Procrastination and picking up my phone when a bug is hard to solve.
• Plan: If I hit a bug and reach for my phone, then I will put my phone in the drawer and write a print statement to debug.
