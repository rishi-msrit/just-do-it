          SWOT Analysis

          What it is

          SWOT Analysis is a classic strategic planning framework designed in the 1960s by Albert Humphrey at the Stanford Research Institute. It helps you assess a personal project, career pivot, or business decision by dividing details into a 2×2 grid. The vertical axis separates Internal Factors (which you control) from External Factors (which the world dictates). The horizontal axis separates Positive Forces (helpful for progress) from Negative Forces (harmful to success).

          The Four Quadrants Explained

          • Strengths (Internal, Positive): Your unique capabilities, assets, resources, skills, and advantages. Ask: What do I do better than anyone else? What resources can I draw upon?
          • Weaknesses (Internal, Negative): Gaps in knowledge, skills, budget, or connections. Ask: What should I improve or avoid? Where do I lack resources or focus?
          • Opportunities (External, Positive): Favorable external trends, openings, technologies, or shifts in your industry. Ask: What interesting trends can I capitalize on? Are there gaps in the market?
          • Threats (External, Negative): External forces, competitor actions, market trends, or economic factors that could derail you. Ask: What obstacles do I face? What are my competitors doing?

          Real-world Examples

          Example A (Deciding on Studying Abroad):
          • Strengths: High grades, language proficiency, adapt easily.
          • Weaknesses: Limited savings, leaving family behind.
          • Opportunities: Global career networks, learning a new culture firsthand.
          • Threats: Volatile currency exchange, visa delays.
          Example B (Launching a Tech Blog):
          • Strengths: Strong technical writing background, deep backend knowledge.
          • Weaknesses: No graphic design skills, hate marketing on social media.
          • Opportunities: High developer interest in simple tutorials, rising popularity of Substack.
          • Threats: Highly saturated niche, AI content generators diluting search traffic.
