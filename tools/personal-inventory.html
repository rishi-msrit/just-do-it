Personal Inventory

What it is

A Personal Inventory is a structured self-audit framework designed to help you map where you stand in life across five key dimensions. By visually organizing your internal assets (Skills, Interests, Values) and external realities (Resources, Constraints) as a horizontal dashboard, you can audit your capacity for new projects and ensure your goals align with reality rather than wishful thinking.

The 5 Core Dimensions

  • Skills (Internal): Things you can execute competently today (e.g., Python coding, accounting, public speaking, copy editing).
  • Interests (Internal): Subjects, problems, or hobbies that naturally capture your curiosity and energize you (e.g., electronic music, urban farming, space exploration).
  • Values (Internal): Core ethical boundaries, principles, or lifestyles that you value most (e.g., creative autonomy, remote schedule flexibility, financial safety, community focus).
  • Resources (External): Tangible assets or networks you can leverage (e.g., 6 months of living expenses saved, a powerful desktop workstation, supportive mentors).
  • Constraints (External): Real-world limitations, bottlenecks, or obligations (e.g., must remain in current city, only have 5 hours free per week, high monthly rent).

How to Fill out this Tool

1. Populate: Add 3-5 tags under each of the five buckets. Be completely honest—do not list skills you wish you had, only those you possess today.

2. Synthesize: Review the resulting dashboard. Look for intersections between your Skills and Interests. Are your goals aligned with your core Values? Are your Constraints going to block your current resource allocation?

Real-world Examples

Example (Evaluating a Freelance Pivot):
• Skills: Technical writing, API documentation, basic SEO.
• Interests: Indiedev, developer tools, blogging.
• Values: High autonomy, work-from-anywhere, low meetings.
• Resources: $10k savings, high-speed home office setup, strong LinkedIn network.
• Constraints: Visa requirements tie me to a registered business, high health insurance cost.
